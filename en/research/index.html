Background of "Future Strategy Research"

Researching differentiated future strategies based on the ‘TJ Park Spirit’ to address the key issues of our future society and propose appropriate responding strategies in order to contribute to creating a new zetigeist

→ Providing forward-looking agenda which connect the present and the future

The Institute will have minimum personnel and focus on planning, management and evaluation to integrate the knowledge of experts in various fields to deal with the core issues of our future society, and provide solutions

Setting up and utilizing comprehensive internal and external networks of experts and research institutes.

Setting up a system in which the institute can cooperate with expert groups to share critical data, suggest new research projects and exchange ideas.
