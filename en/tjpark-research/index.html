TJ Park Research Background of "Future Strategy Research"

POSCO served as the power engine of Korea's industrialization in the late 20th century. Now it is the country's pride as it is the first-class steel company on the spotlight in the world. It was TJ(Tae-Joon) Park who led the company for 25 years from the beginning, never losing interest in or affection for the company in his life. There have been strong research activities on POSCO in the business academic community, but there was practically no research on the spirit and leadership of TJ Park. His biography "TJ Park," a basic text for research on him, was published only in 2004. Since 2010 five books came out, however. They research and assess his life and spirit from various perspectives like philosophy, sociology, psychology, politics, business, and economics. Based on these achievements, we seek to research his spirit and leadership in a more systematic way and utilize them for educational purposes and spread them as social property.
